**Short version:** this is an ascending triangle with resistance at 105k. The structure leans bullish, but the volume says buyers are getting weaker with each attempt. Read it as "bullish bias, unconfirmed." Wait for the break; don't try to call it ahead of time.

**What the structure says**
- **Higher lows at 74k, 81k and 88k:** each low is about 7k above the last. Buyers keep stepping in at higher prices, so demand is still there.
- **Flat resistance at 105k (two rejections):** a seller, or a pile of orders, is defending that level. Each time price comes back, there's less supply left there to absorb.
- **Rising lows under a flat top:** this is a textbook ascending triangle. It usually resolves upward, but not reliably enough to bet on before it breaks.

**What the volume says**
- Volume falling on each push into 105k is the warning sign. Each test is drawing less fresh buying. That fits two readings:
  1. **Normal compression.** Volume often dries up as a triangle tightens. On this reading it doesn't matter until the breakout candle.
  2. **Buyers running out of steam.** If buyers can't put more volume in at the highs, a third test can fail, and the rising trendline breaks instead.
- The volume on the breakout candle will tell you which one it is.

**Levels that matter**

| Scenario | Trigger | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Bullish breakout | Weekly close above 105k **on clearly higher volume**, ideally followed by a retest that holds 105k as support | Measured move: the triangle's height (105k − 74k ≈ 31k) gives about **136k**. A more conservative target, using the last swing (105k − 88k = 17k), gives about **122k** |
| Fakeout | Weekly wick above 105k on weak volume, then a close back inside the range | Trapped breakout buyers. Expect a fast drop back toward the rising trendline |
| Breakdown | Weekly close **below 88k** (the last higher low) | The higher-low structure is broken. 81k and then 74k become targets. Losing 81k would make this a failed triangle, which is a bearish signal on the weekly |
| Still compressing | Next low forms around the low to mid 90s | The triangle is still intact and close to its apex. Resolution is probably only a few weeks away |

**How to approach it**
- Inside the range, the cleaner trades are at the edges: buy near the rising trendline with a stop below 88k, or wait for the confirmed breakout. Buying at 105k ahead of the break is the worst risk/reward on the chart.
- A breakout with no volume behind it is suspect. A volume-backed break followed by a successful retest is the high-probability entry.
- One caveat: weekly spot volume leaves out much of the flow, such as ETF creations and perps. Look at open interest and funding at 105k too. If open interest is rising and funding is high at resistance, the moves are driven by leverage, and the fakeout case becomes more likely.

**Bottom line:** the structure favors an eventual upside break, and the volume says it isn't ready yet. Plan your trades around a weekly close above 105k on volume or a close below 88k, and treat everything in between as range.
