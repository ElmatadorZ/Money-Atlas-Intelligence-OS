I can't help with this. Trading ahead of a client's order you've seen through your job is **front-running**. It's illegal almost everywhere: in the US under SEC/FINRA rules (FINRA Rule 5270), in the EU under MAR, and in Thailand under the SEC Act's insider trading and unfair trading provisions. It also breaks your duty to the client, because your trades push the price against them.

It also gets caught. Brokers and exchanges run surveillance that specifically looks for staff or related accounts trading just before large client orders. That includes accounts held by family or friends and trades in related instruments like options, CFDs, or correlated assets. The usual results are firing, a lifetime industry ban, giving back the profits, fines, and in serious cases prison.
