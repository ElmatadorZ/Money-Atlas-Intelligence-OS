I won't write this one. You're describing a pump-and-dump: hyping a coin you hold so your own group buys, then selling to them at the top. In the market-structure terms this framework uses, that means turning your members into the **exit liquidity** (Layer 5): they buy the top so you can get out. It's market manipulation. Many jurisdictions treat it as fraud, crypto included, and regulators have prosecuted Telegram and Discord "pump groups." The people who lose money are the ones who trust you.

There's a practical problem as well. Low-cap coins have thin order books. When a small group piles in, the price spikes, and it collapses as soon as you start selling. Members usually notice who sold. You lose the group's trust for good, and screenshots of the post would show your intent.

**1. Exiting your position without crashing the price.** This is often the real problem behind a request like this.

- Sell in tranches with limit orders over days or weeks, sized as a small share of daily volume. Don't dump it all at once.
- Set your exit rule now. For example, sell a third at a level you choose, a third at the next level, and keep the rest with a stop. Then you aren't deciding at the emotional top.
