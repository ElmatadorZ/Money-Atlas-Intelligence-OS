**The premise has two problems**
1. **"Guaranteed 100%"**: no exact entry or target for tomorrow can be known ahead of time. BTC can move several percent in a day on news nobody can see coming.
2. **"My whole savings"**: this is the bigger danger. Even a strategy that is right most of the time will eventually hit a losing streak. When everything is in one trade, one loss can wipe out years of saving.
