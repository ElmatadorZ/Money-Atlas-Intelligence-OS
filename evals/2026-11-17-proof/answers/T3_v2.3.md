**GOLD (daily): trade plan**

**Short version:** your chart sends two opposite signals. Don't pick a side yet. Set two conditional orders, one for a breakout above 4,400 and one for a breakdown below 4,310, and let price choose. At 1% risk, both plans come to about **0.01–0.02 lots** on a standard contract.

📍 **SITUATION MAP**
- **Given:** flat resistance at 4,400, rejected 3 times. Higher lows at 4,180, then 4,250, then 4,310. Bearish RSI divergence on the last push. Account 500,000 THB, 1% risk = **5,000 THB per trade**.
- **Premise check:** I'm assuming these levels are XAUUSD in USD/oz. Gold first traded above $4,000 in late 2025, so the levels look plausible. I have no live feed, so check them against your current chart before acting.
- **Not known:** today's price, ATR, the dates of the swings (needed to draw the rising trendline exactly), your broker's contract size and spread.

📍 **SMC LAYER MAP**
Flat highs over rising lows make an **ascending triangle**, which puts price in the **L3 Decision Zone**. Two readings fit:
- **Accumulation:** buyers step in higher each time, and three tests weaken the sellers at 4,400. That points to a breakout (**L2 Expansion**).
- **Distribution:** equal highs at 4,400 leave buy-stop liquidity sitting just above. A spike through 4,400 that fails, together with the RSI divergence, would look like **L4 Distribution**.

The RSI divergence is a warning that momentum is fading. On its own it is not a reason to enter.

📍 **SCENARIOS**

🐂 **Plan A: breakout long**

- **Stop:** **4,295**, under the last higher low. Distance = $110.

- **Note:** the rising trendline drawn through your lows may break *before* 4,310. That gives an earlier but weaker signal.

⚖️ **Base case: no position inside the range.** A triangle that has been tested three times usually resolves soon. Neither signal outweighs the other yet, so the edge is in the break, not in guessing it.

- **If you can't watch daily closes:** only use Plans A and B as alert-then-enter. Don't leave resting stop orders right at 4,400 or 4,310, because that is exactly where false spikes take liquidity.
