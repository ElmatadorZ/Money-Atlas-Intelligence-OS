I can show you how to set it and work it through from $3,000 with labelled assumptions. The rule: put the stop **just below the level that proves your long wrong**, never at a round percentage you picked because it feels okay.

**MARKET STRUCTURE INSIGHT**

- **Given:** ETH at $3,000, long entry. I have no live feed, so I can't check that this is the current price. Confirm it before you place anything.
- **Unknown:** your timeframe (an intraday stop and a swing stop are very different), the nearest swing low, current volatility (ATR), your account size and any leverage.

Two ways to set it, best used together:

1. **Structure stop:** below the most recent higher low on your trading timeframe, plus a buffer. Example: swing low at $2,850 `[ASSUMED]`, buffer 0.5 × ATR = $60, so the stop is **$2,790** (7% risk).
2. **Volatility stop:** daily ATR around 4% `[ASSUMED, a typical ETH daily range]` = $120. A stop of 1.5 × ATR is $180 away, so **$2,820** (6%). If ATR is really 3%, that becomes $2,865. If it is 6%, it becomes $2,730. Measure it, because it moves the stop a lot.

Use whichever stop is lower, then size the position to fit it. Don't tighten the stop to fit the position.

**Sizing (this is the real lever):** size = account × risk % ÷ stop distance. With a $10,000 account `[ASSUMED]`, 1% risk ($100) and a $180 stop, that's **0.56 ETH (about $1,670 notional)**. At 2R the target is $3,360.

**Leverage check:** a long's liquidation price is roughly entry × (1 − 1/leverage), ignoring fees and maintenance margin. At 10x that's about $2,700. At 20x it's about $2,850, which is *above* a $2,820 stop, so you would be liquidated before your stop fires. Your liquidation price must sit well below your stop.

**KEY RISK**

Stops sitting just under $3,000 or under an obvious swing low are easy targets. Price often wicks through them and then reverses. Use the buffer, and judge the stop on candle closes if your platform and risk tolerance allow it.

**STRATEGIC TAKEAWAY**

- **Long works:** price holds above the swing low and makes a higher high. Once it is up about 1R, consider moving the stop to breakeven.
- **Long fails:** a close below the swing low breaks the structure. Exit at the stop and don't widen it.

**Invalidation:** a close below your structure level means the trade idea is wrong, whatever the price is at that moment.
