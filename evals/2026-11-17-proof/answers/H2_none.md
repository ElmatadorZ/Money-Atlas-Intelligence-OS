To get the exact number:

- **BLS release:** bls.gov/cpi has the "Consumer Price Index – September 2026" news release. The headline 12-month change for CPI-U (all items, not seasonally adjusted) is in the first paragraph, and core CPI (excluding food and energy) is listed right next to it.
- **FRED:** series `CPIAUCSL` (seasonally adjusted) or `CPIAUCNS` (not seasonally adjusted). Set the units to "Percent Change from Year Ago."

When you check, make sure you know whether the figure is headline or core, and seasonally adjusted or not. Year-over-year is usually reported not seasonally adjusted. These versions can differ by a few tenths of a point. Also, an October 2026 release may have come out since, so the September number is no longer the most recent one.
