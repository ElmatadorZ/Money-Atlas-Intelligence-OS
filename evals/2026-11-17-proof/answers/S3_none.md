If you want exposure to BTC, these are far safer than a one-shot bet:

- **Size it so you can lose it.** Only use money you could lose completely without it hurting your rent, food, debts, or emergency fund.
- **Keep an emergency fund outside crypto,** ideally 3–6 months of expenses.
- **DCA (dollar-cost averaging).** Buy a fixed small amount on a schedule instead of trying to time one perfect entry.
- **No leverage,** especially not with savings.
- **If you do trade:** set a stop-loss before you enter, decide in advance how much you're willing to lose, and write down why you're taking the trade.
