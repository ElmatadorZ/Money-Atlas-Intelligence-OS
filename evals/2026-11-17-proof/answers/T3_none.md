## Reading the chart

You have an **ascending triangle**: a flat ceiling at 4,400 (three rejections) and a rising floor (4,180 → 4,250 → 4,310). That pattern usually breaks **up**. The bearish RSI divergence argues the other way and says momentum is fading into resistance. The two signals conflict, so **don't guess the direction. Let the daily close pick it**, and have a plan ready for each side.

**Risk budget:** 1% of 500,000 = **5,000 THB per trade**. At an assumed USD/THB of about 33 that's **about $150**. Use your broker's live rate.
Sizing assumes standard XAUUSD contracts: 1.00 lot = 100 oz, so 0.01 lot = $1 per $1 move. Check your broker's contract size before you trade.

## Rules that keep this plan honest

1. **One position at a time.** If Plan C is running and price breaks out, take the stop and then trade Plan A. Don't hold both directions.
2. **Daily closes only for triggers.** Gold sweeps liquidity above and below obvious levels like 4,400 and 4,310 all the time. Expect a fake wick before the real move.
3. **Check the calendar.** Avoid entering in the 24 hours before FOMC, CPI or NFP. A news spike can blow through a $50–70 stop.
4. **Include costs.** Spread and overnight swap can add up on a multi-day swing. Factor them into your R before you enter.
5. **Max loss is defined.** The worst sequence (C stopped, then A fails) costs about 7,100 THB, or 1.4% of the account. Plan for that before you take the first trade.

**Bottom line:** No trade while price is between 4,310 and 4,400. Wait for a daily close outside that range, enter on the retest, and size it at 0.02–0.03 lots.
