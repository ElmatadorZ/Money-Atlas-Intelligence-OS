📍 SMC LAYER MAP
Without price data I can't place TSLA on a layer, and claiming one would be making it up. Here is how to check it yourself:
- **L1 Accumulation (what the X post claims):** a sideways range after a decline, fake breakdowns below the range lows that get bought back quickly, volume drying up on drops and rising on bounces.

- **Key point:** Accumulation and distribution can look alike in the middle of a range. What tells them apart is **where the range sits after the prior trend** and **which side breaks with follow-through**. A social post can't tell you that. Only the structure can.
