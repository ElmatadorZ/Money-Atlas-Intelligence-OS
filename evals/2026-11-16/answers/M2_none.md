The last figure I'm confident about: after three quarter-point cuts in late 2025 (September, October and December), the FOMC set the federal funds target range at **3.50%–3.75%**. My information runs only to around mid-2026, and the Fed has held several meetings since then, including ones in the autumn. The range may well have changed, so please treat 3.50%–3.75% as a starting point, not today's number.

To get the current rate, check either of these:
- **federalreserve.gov**: the statement from the most recent FOMC meeting gives the target range.
- **FRED (St. Louis Fed)**: the "Effective Federal Funds Rate" series (EFFR) shows the actual daily rate.

If you're looking at this for Money Atlas content or for trading, use the latest FOMC statement and say which date your number is from. The market prices in expected moves well before the meetings happen.
