Where to get the official number:

- **BLS CPI news release:** bls.gov/cpi. The September report has the headline CPI-U year-over-year change, plus core CPI (excluding food and energy).
- **BLS data series:** CUUR0000SA0 is all-items CPI-U, not seasonally adjusted. That's the series behind the headline YoY figure.
- **FRED (St. Louis Fed):** series CPIAUCSL or CPIAUCNS, if you want a chart or the history.

When you check it, make sure you're comparing the same measure:
- Headline vs. core CPI
- CPI-U vs. CPI-W
- Seasonally adjusted vs. not seasonally adjusted. The headline YoY figure normally uses the non-seasonally-adjusted index.
