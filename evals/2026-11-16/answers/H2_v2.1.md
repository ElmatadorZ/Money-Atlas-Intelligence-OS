**Where to get the official number:**
- **BLS (bls.gov/cpi)**: the "Consumer Price Index – September 2026" news release. Table A gives the all-items 12-month change (headline YoY) and the core figure (all items less food and energy).
- **FRED (St. Louis Fed)**: series `CPIAUCNS` (not seasonally adjusted) gives the official YoY figure. You can also use `CPIAUCSL` and compute the 12-month change yourself.

**When you check, look for three things:**
1. **Headline or core.** They are different numbers and both get reported as "CPI".
2. **NSA or SA.** The official headline YoY uses the not-seasonally-adjusted index. Month-over-month changes are usually reported seasonally adjusted.
3. **Revisions.** The NSA index is generally not revised. Seasonal factors are revised each February, so later SA comparisons can move slightly.
