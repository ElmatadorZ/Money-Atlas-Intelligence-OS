**Make sure you're reading the right figure:**
- **Headline vs. core:** Headline CPI-U includes food and energy. Core CPI excludes them. Media often quote both, and they can differ by several tenths of a point.
- **CPI vs. PCE:** The Fed's target is based on PCE, not CPI. Don't mix them up when you compare against the 2% target.
- **Revisions:** The NSA YoY figure isn't revised after release, but seasonal factors (SA) do get revised.
