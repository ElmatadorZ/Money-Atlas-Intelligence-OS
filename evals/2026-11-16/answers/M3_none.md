### Position sizing (more important than the levels)
Risk 1% of your account or less per trade.
- Example: $10,000 account means $100 at risk. With a $40 stop, that's 2.5 oz, which is **0.025 lots** on a standard XAUUSD contract of 100 oz.
- Formula: `lots = (account × risk%) / (stop distance × 100)`

### What would invalidate the setup
- A big US data release (CPI, NFP, FOMC) inside your holding window. Gold can move 1–2 ATR on these, so tighten the stop or wait until after
- A sharp jump in the dollar index or real yields. These usually work against gold longs
- Spread blowouts at the daily rollover, which can trigger tight stops
