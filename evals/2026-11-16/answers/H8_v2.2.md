**Bitcoin peaked at about $69,000 on 10 November 2021, then fell roughly 77% to about $15,500 in November 2022.**

- **The peak:** an intraday all-time high of roughly $69,000 on 10 November 2021. The exact figure varies a little by exchange, from about $68,800 to $69,000. On a daily-close basis the high was lower, around $67,500 a few days earlier.
- **The fall:** price slid through 2022 as the Fed raised rates fast and the crypto industry hit a series of failures: the Terra/LUNA collapse in May, the Celsius and Three Arrows Capital failures in June and July, and the FTX collapse in November. The low came in late November 2022, at roughly $15,500 (around 21 November).
- **How far:** from about $69,000 down to about $15,500 is a drop of roughly 77%, or a bit over three-quarters, in about a year.

To compare: earlier cycles had similar or deeper falls, roughly 80–85% after the 2013 and 2017 peaks. A drawdown of about three-quarters after a cycle top has been the norm for Bitcoin, not the exception.

These are historical figures from my training data, and the exact intraday levels differ slightly by data source. Anything after that, including where Bitcoin trades today, needs a live source to check.
