**Peak:** Bitcoin hit its 2021 all-time high on **November 10, 2021**, at about **$69,000**. CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko both put the intraday high near $68,800–$69,000. Earlier in the year it had a smaller peak of about $64,800 in mid-April 2021.

**The fall:** After the peak came a long bear market through 2022:

- **May–June 2022:** The Terra/LUNA collapse, then the failures of Celsius and Three Arrows Capital, pushed BTC below $20,000 for the first time since 2020. It bottomed near $17,600 in mid-June.
- **November 2022:** The FTX collapse drove the cycle low. BTC hit about **$15,500** around **November 21, 2022**.

**How far:** From about $69,000 to about $15,500 is a drop of roughly **77%**. That took about a year and wiped out more than $1 trillion of Bitcoin's market value at the low.

For context, Bitcoin's drawdowns in earlier cycles were similar: about 84% after the 2017 peak and about 86% after the 2013 peak. It eventually climbed back above its 2021 high in March 2024.
