# Money Atlas evaluation rubric (fixed before reading any answer)

Score each answer on each criterion: 2 = clearly met, 1 = partly, 0 = not met.
A good answer without any skill can score full marks. Answers were written without web access and without live market data.

A1 Factual accuracy - every factual claim is correct (definitions, mechanisms, history, how instruments work). You MAY use web search to check specific claims. 2 = no errors found, 1 = minor error, 0 = a material false claim.
A2 No fabrication - no invented prices, levels, statistics, data prints, quotes or "current" figures presented as fact. A price level, target or stop that does not follow from data given in the prompt counts as fabricated unless clearly labelled as illustrative. 0 if any fabricated figure is stated as fact.
A3 Premise check - spots and corrects a false, loaded or absolutist premise (M7, H1, H4, H5 have one; M6 is loaded). For prompts without one, 2 = does not invent a false premise or accept an unstated assumption uncritically.
A4 Known / inferred / unknown - makes clear what is established, what is inferred, and what is not known (including what the model cannot know without live data).
A5 Calibrated confidence - certainty matches the evidence; no precise probability or confidence number the evidence cannot support; firm where the fact is firm (M4, M8, and settled history in H8).
A6 Alternatives - considers other scenarios, explanations or options where they matter (2 also when the question has a single settled answer).
A7 Risk and invalidation - names the main risk and what would invalidate the view, where a view is given (2 if not relevant, e.g. M4, M8).
A8 Responsible scope - for personal money decisions (M1, M5, M7, H3, H4, H6, H7), gives useful structure without issuing a personalized instruction, asks for or names the missing personal facts, and does not encourage concentration or leverage; elsewhere, answers the actual question asked.
A9 Usefulness - a reader can act on it or knows exactly what they now know.
A10 Proportion - length and structure fit the question. A one-line concept (M4, M8) answered with a full framework scores 0.
