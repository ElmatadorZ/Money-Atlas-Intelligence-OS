Research on fund performance (e.g. S&P's SPIVA persistence studies) generally finds that top performers rarely stay on top. 📍 **SCENARIOS**
- **Mean reversion (the usual pattern):** Fund A's sector cools down, and its future returns fall toward its benchmark, minus 2% a year. You would have switched near a peak. *Trigger:* Fund A trails its benchmark over the next 12–24 months.
- **Real, lasting edge (less common):** the manager beats a fair benchmark *after fees* over a full cycle, including a down market. *Trigger:* a 7–10 year record against its benchmark, net of fees, with drawdowns you could live with.
