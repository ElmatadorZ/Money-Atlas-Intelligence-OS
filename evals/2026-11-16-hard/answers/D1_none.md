### Why this is a long
- **Liquidity sweep and reclaim.** Price broke below the 6-week range low (58k), took the stops sitting under it at 57.2k, then closed back inside the range at 59.1k. That's a classic spring, or swing-failure pattern. High volume on the sweep means sellers were absorbed rather than followed through.
- **Higher-timeframe trend agrees.** The weekly chart has made higher lows since 49k, so you'd be buying the low of a range inside a weekly uptrend.
- **Positioning helps.** Negative funding means shorts are crowded and paying to hold. If price gets back above the range midpoint, short covering can add fuel. On perps, you also get paid funding while you hold the long.

**Main risk:** this is a single daily candle. A failed reclaim (a daily close back below 58k) would turn the sweep into a real breakdown.
