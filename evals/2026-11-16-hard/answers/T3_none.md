# Gold (Daily): Trade Plan

## 1. What the chart is saying

- **Structure:** A flat ceiling at 4,400 (tested 3 times) with higher lows at 4,180, 4,250 and 4,310 makes an **ascending triangle**. Textbook, that's a bullish continuation pattern: buyers keep stepping in higher while sellers defend one price.
- **The conflict:** Bearish RSI divergence on the last push means momentum is weakening even though price came back to the same level. Buyers are reaching 4,400 with less force each time.
- **Read:** The pattern leans up and momentum leans down. **I'd treat this as a coiled range and not guess the direction.** You trade the break, not the forecast. The range is narrowing (the gap between lows and the ceiling shrinks from 220 to 90 points), so a decisive move is getting close.

Things this plan can't know: live price, the current USD/THB rate, your broker's contract specs, and upcoming news. Check each before entering.

## 2. Risk budget

- Account: 500,000 THB × 1% = **5,000 THB maximum risk per trade**
- In USD (illustrative rate 33 THB/USD, **use the live rate**): 5,000 / 33 ≈ **$151**
- XAUUSD standard contract (check with your broker): 1 lot = 100 oz, so **0.01 lot = $1 per $1 move**

**Sizing formula:**
`Lots = (5,000 / USDTHB) ÷ (stop distance in $ × 100)`

Always round lots **down**. Set the stop where the structure says it goes, then size the position to fit it. Never move the stop to fit a position size.

## 4. Execution rules

1. **Daily close confirms.** Intraday pokes above 4,400 have failed 3 times already.
2. **One position at a time.** If A triggers, B is off, and the reverse. Never hold both.
3. **Check the news calendar** (FOMC, CPI, NFP, Fed speakers) before entering. Gold breakouts on news often whipsaw, and a gap can jump past your stop.
4. **Account for spread and swap.** A wide spread at entry eats into the stop distance, so recompute the size with the actual fill.
5. **If you're stopped out, accept it.** Wait for the structure to reset before re-entering, and don't double the size to "win it back."
