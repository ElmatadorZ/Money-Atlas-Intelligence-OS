📍 **FIRST PRINCIPLE BREAKDOWN**

- **Problem:** Buyers and sellers are fighting at one price. Sellers defend 4,400. Buyers keep paying more on each dip.
- **Cause:** Each higher low means demand is coming in earlier. Each rejection at 4,400 means supply there hasn't run out yet.
- **Mechanism:** Every test of a ceiling uses up some of the supply sitting there. Meanwhile, stops pile up just above 4,400 (from shorts at the triple top) and just below 4,310 (from longs at the last higher low). Price is drawn to those stop pools.
- **Leverage:** The asymmetry is in the **reaction at a boundary**, not in the middle of the range. Entering between 4,310 and 4,400 means a wide stop and no edge.
- **Outcome:** The two signals conflict. The pattern leans bullish (ascending triangle). Momentum leans bearish (divergence and triple rejection). **Neither one is enough on its own.** Let price show which side wins.

📍 **SYSTEM MAP**

Macro (unknown) → USD liquidity and real yields → gold demand → price compressing under 4,400

The chart tells you *where* the decision will happen. It doesn't tell you *which way* it goes. A macro catalyst (CPI, FOMC, NFP, geopolitics) will most likely trigger the break. **Check the economic calendar before you place anything.**
